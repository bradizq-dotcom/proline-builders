Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you take on?

We specialize in commercial and multi-family projects — apartment complexes, HOAs, property managers, and developers. Our contracts typically range from $50K to $5M+.

What is your minimum project size?

Our minimum project size is $50,000. We focus on work that requires professional coordination, permitting, and execution — not small residential jobs.

Do you handle permitting?

Yes. We manage the full permitting process — permit acquisition, variance handling, and city inspection coordination are all part of our standard project service.

What areas do you serve?

We work across Florida and have active projects in Nashville, TN. Service area depends on project scope and timeline.

How long does the process take?

Timeline varies by project. A typical re-roofing project takes 2-6 months from contract to completion. New construction and full GC projects run longer depending on scope.

Do you work on occupied buildings?

Yes. Tenant-occupied projects are a specialty. We have protocols for working around residents safely and with minimal disruption.
